# Product direction

A little world that lives alongside your work. The first public prototype shares the existing pasture extension and the experimental whole-window source.

Keep sheep, fish-to-sheep evolution, weather, daylight, Disco, Race, Cozy and God mode. No neglect penalties, streaks or urgent rewards. Growth currently counts visible time only.

Decoration placement, creator packs, sharing arrangements and music integration are future ideas, not release commitments. Start by making the current build understandable and reproducible, and welcome small bug-fix PRs.
