# Asset provenance and reuse

Doodles was made with a personal ChatGPT account using Astra. Code and artwork were created with AI tools, with direction and selection by Shubha Jagannatha. This describes the creation process; it is not a claim that every output is unique or that every generated element independently qualifies for copyright protection.

## Project-created material

The project's own code and assets are offered under the root MIT license to the extent the maintainer holds rights in them. This includes pixel sheep, goldfish and walking atlases, flowers, grass/paper SVGs, fireflies, eyes, disco decorations, procedural clouds/rain, and the campfire SVG frames. Embedded generated art is covered on the same basis. The repository's sheep notes record image-generation prompts; exact model/version records are incomplete. No claim of entirely hand-drawn artwork is made.

The campfire design notes list visual references; reference artwork is not included in the release. Historical reference-photo collections, discarded sprite experiments and raw recordings are excluded.

## Third-party material

Third-party material retains its own terms; the project's MIT license does not replace them. See [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) for sources, creators and licenses.

## Contributions and corrections

Please submit only code/art/audio you have permission to share. Identify its source and license, disclose substantial AI assistance, and include required notices. Flag reference-based or copied material clearly. Small PRs and attribution corrections are welcome; larger additions should start with an issue. If you believe something here should not be included, open an issue identifying the asset and concern without posting private material.
